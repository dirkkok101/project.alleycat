# Batch D — backgrounds

All nine files are in `out/bg/`. Each is an opaque RGB JPEG, 1280×720, saved at quality 85. Style is one muted cel-shaded storybook pass (thick rounded contours, soft shading, low saturation) so gameplay sprites can sit on top. Read-back found no characters, no animals, and no text.

Floor bands that the manifest places at the bottom 12% were measured and fit to that band (86px). The alley sky band measures 20% of the height.
